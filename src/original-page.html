Meet your mentor — Tom

Hey there, I no longer worry about finances and live on my terms — but it took a while to get there.

2017

Trapped in the cycle

Fresh out of university, I landed a full-time job. It paid the bills, but I was constantly tired, creatively drained, and stuck in a routine that didn’t feel like mine.

2019

Side hustles and sacrifices

I started freelancing after work — late nights, weekend gigs, no social life. It brought in extra cash, but I was still trading time for money. I knew there had to be another way.

2020

Discovering digital products

I stumbled on creators selling templates, courses, and ebooks. The idea of building once and earning on repeat felt like a cheat code. I spent months learning everything I could.

2021

Launched my first product

It was messy and far from perfect — but it worked. I made £560 in the first week. Not life-changing, but it changed my mindset completely.

2022

Scaled to 5-figure months

I doubled down. Built more products, automated systems, grew my audience. Within a year, I quit my 9–5 and took my work with me while travelling.

2023

Freedom on all fronts

I was finally in control of my income, schedule, and location. I worked 3–4 hours a day doing what I loved. My digital products earned while I rested.

2024

Launched this course

After helping friends and clients do the same, I decided to package everything I’d learned. This course is the exact roadmap I wish I had when I started — so you can skip the burnout and get straight to building your own freedom.
